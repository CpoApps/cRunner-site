cRunner Privacy Policy

Effective 6 September 2026

cRunner is a private, local-first fitness app. It has no account system, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK, and it does not send workout data to the developer.

Data cRunner handles

While workout setup is open, cRunner briefly receives location fixes to show GPS readiness and estimated accuracy. Preview fixes are not added to a route or counted as distance. When you start a workout, cRunner can record activity type, date, time, duration, precise location, route, distance, speed, pace, elevation, pauses, splits, notes, perceived effort, and recovery data. Apple Watch can also record heart rate during an active workout.

Storage and device transfer

Workout history is stored on your iPhone. Pending Watch workouts stay on your Watch until they transfer to the paired iPhone. cRunner has no developer-operated cloud database. Workout history, recovery journals, queued Watch transfers, and protected on-device backups are excluded from cloud backup. The backup is removed when you delete all app data or the app.

Apple Health

With permission, cRunner writes completed workouts, distance, route, pauses, and available heart-rate samples to Apple Health. The iPhone app does not read Health history. The Watch app reads live workout heart rate only during a workout you start. Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, profiling, or data mining.

Location

Location is accessed while workout setup is open to show GPS readiness and during a workout you start for route, distance, pace, speed, and elevation. Setup updates stop when setup closes or cRunner leaves the foreground. You can change Location and Health permissions in Apple system settings.

User-initiated exports

You can deliberately export a GPX route or portable archive. Portable archives contain dates, workout details, and precise routes, but omit heart-rate samples. You choose the destination. cRunner never uploads or exports workout data automatically.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your devices until you delete workouts, choose Delete all activities, or remove the app. Deleting cRunner data does not delete workouts already written to Apple Health; manage those separately in the Health app.

Security, children, and medical use

cRunner uses Apple platform protections such as app sandboxing, protected files, and permission controls. It is a general fitness tool, not a medical device or emergency service, and is not designed specifically for children.

Contact and changes

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, use the contact details on the cRunner support page.
