Help with iPhone, Apple Watch, GPS, and Apple Health

Apple Watch

Install cRunner from the Watch app on the paired iPhone. The Watch can record without an iPhone or internet. If installation fails, restart both devices, confirm Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, update iOS/watchOS, remove an incomplete installation, and try again. Developer Mode is needed only for locally signed builds installed from Xcode, not for App Store or TestFlight builds.

Location accuracy

Allow location while using cRunner, keep Precise Location enabled, and open workout setup outdoors with a clear view of the sky. Wait for Good or Excellent GPS when possible, then start. Active recordings can continue while the screen is locked.

Apple Health

Enable Health export in cRunner Settings. If access was denied, update cRunner permissions in the Health app. Workouts already written to Health are managed separately there.

Backup and transfer

On-device backups remain on the iPhone and are excluded from cloud backup. Portable archives can be transferred manually and omit heart-rate samples.
